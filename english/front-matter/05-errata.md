# Printing errors

At the page and line indicated, replace the form under **For** with the one under **Read**. Both forms are given in the original language so that even changes in spacing or punctuation can be seen. In the line column, `op de cant` and its abbreviated forms mean “in the margin.” `ibid.` means “in the same place”; `pag.` or `p.` means page, `lin.` or `l.` line, `cap.` chapter, `lib.` book, and `vers.` verse.
