# On the Perseverance of the Saints

Against D. Petrus Bertius’s two treatises on the falling away of the saints

Written by Sebastian Damman, preacher in Zutphen, and published by him for the benefit of ordinary readers.

Romans 16:17–18

> Brothers, I urge you to watch out for those who cause division and put stumbling blocks in people’s way, contrary to the teaching you have learned. Turn away from them. For such people serve not the Lord Jesus Christ but their own belly. With smooth words and praise they lead the hearts of the simple astray.

Printed at Enchuysen by Jacob Lenaertsz Meyn, bookseller on the Nieuwe-straet at the sign of the Gilded Writing Book, in the year 1615.
